A simple example in Kina

For illustration only, using the advertised rates. This is not a promise of earnings.

  • A friend you invited places bets totalling K1,000 in a week. At 1%, that is worth K10 to you.
  • That friend invites someone who bets K1,000 in the same week. At 0.5%, that is worth K5 to you.
  • Total in this example: K15.

The reward comes from the amount bet, not from how much anyone wins or loses, and it depends fully on other people's activity. Some weeks it could be zero.

Inviting people responsibly

  • Invite only adults aged 18 or over.
  • Be honest that gambling carries risk and most people lose money over time.
  • Do not promise income, and do not pressure anyone to deposit.
  • Never tell someone to borrow money or use money meant for bills.
  • Remember that your reward grows when others bet more, so do not encourage anyone to bet beyond a budget.

Papua New Guinea has no domestic licensing regime for online casinos, so check the rules that apply to you before you promote it.

Frequently asked questions

How does the Kaya4U agent program work?

Kaya4U advertises that you earn 1% of the bets made by friends you invite, plus 0.5% of the bets made by the friends they invite. Rewards are described as automatic.

It depends entirely on how much your invited players bet. For example, at the advertised 1% rate, K1,000 in bets from a friend would be worth K10. This is an illustration, not a promise.
